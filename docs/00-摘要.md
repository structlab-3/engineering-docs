# 摘要 / Abstract

## 中文摘要

主动噪声控制技术通过产生与初级噪声幅值相等、相位相反的次级声波实现噪声抵消，在低频噪声控制领域具有独特优势。传统的滤波-x最小均方算法基于高斯噪声假设和均方误差准则设计，在面对脉冲噪声环境时性能急剧下降甚至完全失效。脉冲噪声广泛存在于工业设备冲击、交通工具突发噪声和电子设备电磁干扰等实际场景中，其统计特性服从重尾分布，具有方差无限或不存在的特点。脉冲噪声中的离群值幅值可能是正常信号的数十倍，这些极端值在梯度计算中产生过大扰动，使自适应滤波器权重向量发生剧烈振荡，破坏算法的收敛过程。现有的脉冲噪声主动控制算法虽然从不同角度进行了改进，但仍存在性能不足或计算复杂度过高的问题，特别是在极强脉冲环境下，滤波器系数的累积过冲现象仍然难以有效抑制。

针对上述问题，本文提出两种基于M估计器的脉冲噪声主动控制算法。第一种是增强型基于M估计器的滤波器系数限制滤波-x最小平均M估计算法。该算法在改进型滤波-x最小平均M估计算法基础上引入基于M估计器的权重软约束机制，通过在误差信号、参考信号和滤波器权重三个层面同时应用M估计器保护，形成对脉冲噪声的三重防御体系。权重M估计器采用L1范数作为约束对象，通过Hampel M估计器的非线性变换实现对权重向量的自适应软约束，避免传统硬限幅方法的截断失真。算法所需的所有M估计器阈值参数均通过在线估计方法获得，采用指数加权移动平均估计均值、滑动窗中值滤波估计方差，无需先验信息或离线调试。第二种是增强型滤波-x符号变换最小平均M估计算法。该算法采用符号函数对误差信号进行二值化处理，对参考信号采用M估计器进行非线性变换，形成"符号变换+M估计器"的混合保护机制。符号变换通过消除误差信号的幅值信息从根本上防止误差离群值的过度影响，计算复杂度极低。算法无需权重约束，结构简洁，相比第一种算法计算复杂度降低约40%。

仿真实验在特征指数为1.6、1.4和1.2的三种脉冲强度环境下进行，采用实测的室内环境声学脉冲响应作为初级通道和次级通道模型。实验结果表明，增强型滤波-x符号变换最小平均M估计算法在所有测试场景下均表现最优，稳态降噪量分别达到-16.81 dB、-14.03 dB和-9.78 dB，平均稳态降噪量-13.54 dB，平均收敛时间10033次迭代。该算法相比增强型基于M估计器的滤波器系数限制滤波-x最小平均M估计算法性能提升5.45 dB，收敛速度快18%。符号变换的极端归一化特性在脉冲噪声环境下显示出独特优势，完全消除误差离群值的幅值影响。两种算法的成功验证了脉冲噪声主动控制算法设计既可以通过增加保护层次来提升性能，也可以通过简化结构来获得更优的性能，关键在于根据脉冲噪声的统计特性选择合适的非线性变换方法。

关键词： 主动噪声控制，脉冲噪声，M估计器，符号变换，滤波器系数限制


## English Abstract

Active noise control (ANC) technology achieves noise cancellation by generating secondary sound waves with equal amplitude and opposite phase to the primary noise, offering unique advantages in low-frequency noise control. The traditional filtered-x least mean square (FxLMS) algorithm, designed based on Gaussian noise assumptions and mean square error criterion, suffers from severe performance degradation or complete failure when confronted with impulsive noise environments. Impulsive noise is prevalent in practical scenarios such as industrial equipment impacts, transient bursts from transportation vehicles, and electromagnetic interference from electronic devices. Its statistical characteristics follow heavy-tailed distributions with infinite or non-existent variance. Outliers in impulsive noise can have amplitudes tens of times larger than normal signals, causing excessive disturbances in gradient calculations that lead to violent oscillations in adaptive filter weight vectors and destroy the convergence process. Although existing impulsive noise ANC algorithms have made improvements from different perspectives, they still suffer from insufficient performance or excessive computational complexity, particularly in extremely strong impulsive environments where the cumulative overshoot phenomenon of filter coefficients remains difficult to suppress effectively.

To address these issues, this thesis proposes two M-estimator-based impulsive noise active control algorithms. The first is an enhanced M-estimator-based weight-constrained filtered-x least mean M-estimate (EMCFxLMM) algorithm. Building upon the modified filtered-x least mean M-estimate algorithm, this algorithm introduces an M-estimator-based soft weight constraint mechanism, forming a triple defense system against impulsive noise by simultaneously applying M-estimator protection at three levels: error signal, reference signal, and filter weights. The weight M-estimator uses L1 norm as the constraint object and achieves adaptive soft constraint on weight vectors through nonlinear transformation of the Hampel M-estimator, avoiding truncation distortion caused by traditional hard-limiting methods. All M-estimator threshold parameters required by the algorithm are obtained through online estimation methods, using exponentially weighted moving average for mean estimation and sliding window median filtering for variance estimation, without requiring prior information or offline tuning. The second is an enhanced filtered-x sign least mean M-estimate (EMFxSLMS) algorithm. This algorithm employs the sign function for binary processing of error signals and applies M-estimator for nonlinear transformation of reference signals, forming a hybrid protection mechanism of "sign transformation + M-estimator". Sign transformation fundamentally prevents excessive influence of error outliers by eliminating amplitude information, with extremely low computational complexity. The algorithm requires no weight constraint and has a simple structure, reducing computational complexity by approximately 40% compared to the first algorithm.

Simulation experiments were conducted under three impulsive intensity environments with characteristic exponents of 1.6, 1.4, and 1.2, using measured indoor acoustic impulse responses as primary and secondary channel models. Experimental results demonstrate that the EMFxSLMS algorithm achieves optimal performance in all test scenarios, with steady-state noise reductions of -16.81 dB, -14.03 dB, and -9.78 dB respectively, an average steady-state noise reduction of -13.54 dB, and an average convergence time of 10,033 iterations. Compared to the EMCFxLMM algorithm, this algorithm improves performance by 5.45 dB and converges 18% faster. The extreme normalization characteristic of sign transformation demonstrates unique advantages in impulsive noise environments, completely eliminating the amplitude effects of error outliers. The success of both algorithms validates that impulsive noise ANC algorithm design can achieve performance improvement either by adding protection layers or by simplifying structure to obtain superior performance, with the key lying in selecting appropriate nonlinear transformation methods based on the statistical characteristics of impulsive noise.

Keywords: active noise control, impulsive noise, M-estimator, sign transformation, weight constraint
